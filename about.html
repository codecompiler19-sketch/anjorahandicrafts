The Spirit of Craft

Craft Beyond Borders

"Anjora" in Sanskrit symbolises light, dawn, and radiance. We bring the luminous creativity of India's village craftspeople into modern homes across the globe.

Who We Are

Founded with a deep reverence for indigenous Indian artistry, Anjora Handicrafts bridges rural master craft clusters with art lovers, interior decorators, and mindful consumers worldwide. Every piece in our collection is not just an object of beauty, but a living relic of ancestral craft methods passed down through generations.

Our Craft Clusters

We collaborate directly with recognized craft guilds across India:

  • Moradabad, Uttar Pradesh: Renowned as the "Brass City" (Peetal Nagri), where lost-wax casting and exquisite hand-engraving bring spiritual deities and temple urlis to life.
  • Mithila, Bihar: Where women folk artists craft authentic Madhubani paintings using bamboo twigs and organic plant pigments.
  • Jaipur, Rajasthan: Renowned for GI-tagged Blue Pottery, hand block-printed mulmul dohars, and royal lac bangles.
  • Saharanpur, Uttar Pradesh: Famed for master woodcarvers shaping seasoned Sheesham and mango timber into intricate furniture and jharokhas.
  • Kashmir Valley: Master craftspersons producing hand-hammered copper samovars and world-famous Sozni needlework Pashmina shawls.

Ethical Fair Trade Promise

In conventional commerce, middlemen pocket up to 80% of retail margins while village craftspeople struggle to sustain their craft. At Anjora Handicrafts:

  • Craft workers receive fair, prompt compensation upfront for raw materials and labor.
  • Royalties from every sale fund community workshops and craft tool apprenticeships.
  • We preserve endangered art styles that are otherwise in danger of mechanization.

"When you hold a piece from Anjora, you are holding the fingerprints of a master who has devoted their entire lifetime to mastering an ancient flame."
